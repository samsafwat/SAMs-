# The Shenzhen Summit — Treatment

Style: **Broadcast News Package** (`news-package`), English. About 58 s, 16:9, 24 fps.
Channel: **WORLD DESK** (fictional), accent lemon `#F2CF1D` on ink.

Brief deviation, at the user's request: the user asked for "hyper-realistic, real human presence". So the picture layer carries
(a) **real file photos** of the three leaders (Wikimedia Commons, public domain / CC BY 4.0), tagged `FILE PHOTO · <year>`, and
(b) **photoreal AI-generated fictional people** (Magnific), tagged `ILLUSTRATION · AI-GENERATED` the whole time they are on screen.
Generated people are viewers around the world, never the leaders, never the event. No generated anchor.

## 1. Three candidate structures

1. **Round-up**: rundown of four stories of the day (summit, Ukraine strikes, ICJ ruling, COP31) and tick them off. Broad, but the summit gets 12 s and no humans.
2. **One lead package, bookended by a viewer**: a cold open on one person reading the headline. Then sting → what → who → what was signed → what was left out → the quote → the world watching → back to the same person.
3. **Countdown "3 leaders, 4 promises, 2 omissions, 1 question"**: punchy, but the numbers game trivialises a nuclear-risk story.

**Chosen: 2.** The user wants human presence, and a viewer framing gives it a reason to exist: the summit is about leaders, the film is about the people who live with the outcome. The ending echoes the opening.

## 2. Logline and arc

Three of the world's most powerful men sit at one table for the first time, and the world leans in to read what they signed.
- **Setup**: a woman in a rainy city stops to read one headline.
- **Turn**: the declaration's promises tick in, then the omissions. Silence, then the stamp NO BREAKTHROUGH YET.
- **Ending**: faces across the world watching. Back to the woman, who looks up. "The world will be the judge."

## 3. Benchmark

Learn: evening-news package grammar (strap, bug, one fact per screen, the dated eyebrow line, wipes on the beat).
Don't learn: virtual sets, anchors, channel idents, crawls, any real broadcaster's look.

## 5. Beat sheet / voice (Kokoro English, one narrator)

- 0.3 VO1 "Tonight, much of the world is reading one headline."
- 5.8 VO2 "On November nineteenth, in Shenzhen, the leaders of the United States, China and Russia met together, for the first time."
- 12.3 VO3 "Donald Trump. Xi Jinping. Vladimir Putin. 'We're making history,' Trump said."
- 19.3 VO4 "Their joint peace declaration backs stable energy flows, a dialogue on nuclear risks, and strategic stability. And it warns international courts not to abuse their positions."
- 29.3 VO5 "What it left out: no timetable for arms-control talks. And, reports say, no word on the war in Ukraine."
- 35.3 VO6 "Xi Jinping put it plainly: 'Nuclear war cannot be won, and must not be fought.'"
- 41.3 VO7 "For billions watching, from cafés to container ports, one question remains: will these words become action?"
- 49.3 VO8 "'No peace was ever forged by declining opportunities,' Trump said. The world will be the judge."

## 6. Cue map

120 BPM, beat = 0.5 s; bar = 2 s. Beat 0 at 5.5 s (end of the sting).
- 0–4: rain ambience and a low drone, no beat (a cold open).
- 4.0: sting tutti hit. 5.5 the theme starts: pizzicato root-fifth, kick 1 & 3, a saw pulse.
- 12.0 / 13.0 / 14.0: the three cards land on beats; timpani + glock on each.
- 19.0: band wipe + whoosh. Ticks at 22.0, 24.0, 26.0, 27.5 (on beats), click + glock each.
- 29.0: hard cut + timpani. Music thins to the drone. **32.5–33.0: full silence (1 beat).** 33.0: stamp, the heaviest hit, then a held drone.
- 35.0: quote card; pads only (the breath).
- 41.0: theme returns in a major-key lift; cuts at 41, 43, 45, 47 (every bar), soft hit each.
- 49.0: band wipe; the music resolves. 55.0: end-card tutti; ring-out to 58.

## 8. Type and titles

Barlow Condensed 800 (names, numbers, the plate), Barlow Condensed 600 (secondary), IBM Plex Mono 500 (datelines, sources, tags). English only on screen; Chinese lines from the style's demo are dropped (the film's language is English). No burned-in subtitles; `shenzhen-summit.srt` ships beside the film.

## 9. Deviations from the style, and why

- **Colour plates, not the duotone grade.** The user asked for hyper-realistic, attractive pictures with real people, so the photoreal plates keep their colour (mild contrast, vignette, light grain) instead of the style's greyscale duotone.
- **Generated people.** The style's prompts normally exclude people. Here the people in the plates are AI-generated and fictional, at the user's request, and tagged `ILLUSTRATION · AI-GENERATED · FICTIONAL` for every second they are on screen. They play viewers, never the leaders or the event.
- **Real photos of the leaders** come from Wikimedia Commons (public domain / CC BY 4.0), tagged `FILE PHOTO` with source and year: they are not from the summit itself.
- **English only** on screen; the strap has one headline line and one secondary line.
- **Retimed to the voice**: final length 72 s (the narration ran longer than the first plan). Final times are in `main.js` (`SCENES`, `WIPES`, `VO_AT`).
