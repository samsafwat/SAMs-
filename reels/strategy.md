# SAM — content strategy (the "brand bible")

Every skill in the daily pipeline reads this file. Edit it to steer the channel; `reel-analytics`
appends what the numbers teach us to `reels/learnings.md`, and the scriptwriter reads both.

## Positioning

**One AI trick a day that saves you an hour.** Simple enough to try in 60 seconds, specific
enough that viewers haven't seen it in ten other reels. Bilingual: English for global reach,
Egyptian Arabic (عامية مصرية) for the home audience, where very few creators do this well.

The promise in every reel: *you'll be able to do this tonight.* No hype, no "AI will replace
you", no vague lists of 50 tools.

## Scene grammar (what the engine can draw)

`hook` · `presenter` · `tool` · `steps` · `list` · `strikes` · `stat` · `quote` · `voice` · `cta`
(see `reels/engine/README.md`). A typical 30–45 s reel:

1. `hook` (0–3 s) — the scroll-stopper.
2. `tool` or `stat` — what it is / why it matters.
3. `steps` — the exact how-to (max 3 steps).
4. `list` — 2–3 pro tricks (the "exclusive" part).
5. `quote` or `strikes` — the memorable line.
6. `cta` — follow + save.

Use `presenter` (your face) as scene 2 or 6 at least 3× a week: faces build a following,
text-only reels build views.

## Hook formulas (rotate; never reuse the same one two days running)

- **Stop doing X** — "Stop reading PDFs." / "بطّل تكتب الإيميلات بنفسك."
- **Nobody tells you** — "Nobody tells you ChatGPT can do this."
- **Price anchor** — "This $0 tool replaced a $30 subscription."
- **Time anchor** — "I did 3 hours of work in 4 minutes."
- **Wrong way / right way** — "You're prompting it wrong."
- **Curiosity gap** — "Google hid this button."

Rules: the hook's first line is ≤ 4 words on screen; the spoken hook is ≤ 12 words; the
payoff of the hook must arrive in the reel (no clickbait the content can't pay).

## Script rules

- 30–45 seconds. 80–110 spoken words in English; Arabic usually needs ~15% more time.
- One idea per reel. If it needs a second idea, it's tomorrow's reel.
- Every claim must be true **today**: verify on the tool's official site/changelog and store
  the source URL in the spec (`sources`). Never invent features, prices or numbers.
- Say the tool name out loud and show it on screen; show the URL when there is one.
- Egyptian Arabic is spoken, warm, slightly playful: "خلّي"، "ببلاش"، "حركة"، "دوس". Keep
  tool names and UI labels in English (that's what people see on screen).
- End with one CTA, not three. Alternate "follow" and "save/share" CTAs.

## Visual identity (don't drift)

Dark ink background, coral accent (#d97757), violet blobs, film grain, heavy display type,
word-by-word captions with the active word in coral, "✳ @SAM" watermark. Hook slam with flash
on frame 1. Each reel has one surprise moment (strike-through, number count-up, 3D card flip).

## Posting

- Daily, both languages, at the best time Metricool reports for each network (fallback:
  19:30 Cairo time for Arabic, 17:00 Cairo time for English).
- Caption: one line hook + 3 short lines of value + CTA + 3–5 hashtags (no hashtag walls).
- Always declare AI content where the platform asks (Instagram `isAiGenerated`,
  TikTok `isAigc`, YouTube `isAiGeneratedContent`): the voice is cloned and the video is
  generated. Undeclared synthetic media can get posts down-ranked or removed.
- Reply to the first 20 comments within an hour (the routine drafts replies; you send them).

## What "growth" means here

There is no guaranteed path to millions of followers, and no tool can promise virality. What
reliably compounds: posting daily for 90+ days, a recognisable format, hooks that are tested
and iterated weekly, and a few collaborations. `reel-analytics` tracks hook retention, saves and
shares per pillar, and shifts the mix toward what works.
